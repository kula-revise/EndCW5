What You Have Now

You have a full answer to a real NSC question, built decision by decision rather than copied.

The next step is not another lesson. It is a blank page, a pen and 45 minutes. Write this question out under time, without looking at the essay you built. Then compare the two and find out which paragraph you cannot yet produce from memory. That paragraph is your revision.

One last thing. Notice how often the option that lost marks was the one that simply told the story. Description is the most expensive habit in History. You have spent five lessons breaking it.
